TYPOGRAPHY

Use a sophisticated serif typeface for major headings.

Preferred heading font:
- Cormorant Garamond
OR
- DM Serif Display
OR
- Playfair Display

Use a clean, modern sans-serif for body text:
- Inter
OR
- Manrope
OR
- DM Sans

For small decorative/editorial labels, use a handwritten/script font sparingly:
- Allura
OR
- Great Vibes
OR
- Parisienne

IMPORTANT TYPOGRAPHY RULE:
Do not use the script font for normal content.

Use the serif font for:
- Hero headings
- Section headings
- Book titles
- Editorial quotes
- Major feature headings

Use the sans-serif font for:
- Navigation
- Buttons
- Descriptions
- Metadata
- Forms
- Supporting text

Use the script font only for:
- Small eyebrow labels
- Decorative words such as "New Release", "Featured", "Written by", "Explore", etc.
